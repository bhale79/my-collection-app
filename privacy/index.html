Privacy Policy

Effective date: to be announced at launch

The Rail Roster ("we," "us") is the operator of this Service. This policy explains what information the Service touches and where it lives.

The short version

  • Your collection data and photos live in YOUR Google account — your Google Sheet, your Google Drive. We don't have a database of your trains.
  • We keep only what's needed to run your account: your email address and your subscription/trial status.
  • We never see your card number. Payments are handled entirely by Stripe.
  • We don't sell or rent your information. Ever. There are no ads.

What we store, and why

  • What / Where / Why
  • Your email address and name — Our account list (a private spreadsheet) — To know who you are when you sign in, and whether your trial or subscription is active
  • Trial start date / subscription status — Same account list — To run the 30-day trial and yearly subscription
  • Community catalog contributions (optional, anonymous) — Our shared reference data — If you opt in: item numbers, conditions, and identification results — never tied to your name or email — to grow the collector-built catalog

What stays in YOUR Google account

Your items, variations, conditions, prices paid, estimated values, locations, notes, want lists, sales records, contacts you add, and every photo. The Service reads and writes this data only at your direction, using the Google permissions you grant at sign-in. Revoke access anytime at myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party access; everything remains in your account.

Google API disclosure: The Rail Roster's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We access your Google Sheets and Drive solely to provide the collection-tracking features you see, never for advertising, and we don't transfer that data to third parties except as needed to provide those features or as required by law.

Photo identification

When you use photo identification, the photo you submit is sent to an automated photo-reading service (Google's) to identify the item, under the consent you gave during setup. Photos are used for identification only. You can decline this feature and use manual entry, barcodes, or label reading instead.

Payments

Handled by Stripe, Inc. When you subscribe, you give your card details to Stripe directly — they never pass through us. Stripe tells us only who paid and until when. Stripe's privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy.

Cookies and tracking

The Service keeps sign-in and app settings on your own device (local storage). We don't run third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Deleting your information

Email us at support@therailroster.com and we'll remove your email and account status from our records. Your collection needs no deletion request — it's already in your own Google account, under your control.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes materially, we'll notify you in the app or by email before the change takes effect. Questions: support@therailroster.com.
