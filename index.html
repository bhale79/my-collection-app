What it does

Everything a collection needs — in one roster

Catalog & identify in seconds

  • Look up any piece against a 60,000+ item master list — Lionel (pre-war, postwar & modern), MTH (O, HO, G, S & Tinplate), Atlas, RMT, Weaver, and more.
  • Don't know the number? Identify it by photo, scan the box barcode, or read the printed label — right from your phone.
  • Automatic variation matching — pick the exact paint/road/box version, with reference links to the source.
  • Growing through the community every week — new finds get added; new makers get their own home in the app.

Organized the way collectors think

  • Smart grouping — engines with their tenders, AA/AB/ABA diesel sets, boxes, and instruction sheets linked together, not scattered.
  • Track where everything is — totes, shelves, rooms, storage units — so you can actually find a piece later.
  • Photograph every angle, stored safely in your own Google Drive.

Know what you have

  • Record condition, what you paid, and your estimated values — with totals across your whole collection.
  • One-tap Collection Report — print or PDF, with photos and totals — a detailed inventory you can share with whoever you choose — friends, family, auction house, or insurance agent.
  • Price research, made easier — reference links for your exact variation, plus one-tap searches of current and sold listings.

Buy smart, sell smart

  • Want & Upgrade lists — track what you're hunting, with target condition and max price.
  • Duplicate Checker — never buy the same car twice.
  • Set Builder & Companion Suggester — see which sets you can complete and which engines are missing their tender or B-unit.
  • List items for sale, record sales — your collection doubles as your sales ledger, with clean shareable PDFs.

Built for the hobby, anywhere

  • Works on your phone at the show or your desktop at home — installs like an app right from your browser, no app store needed.
  • Take your lists to the show — your roster, want list, parts list, and dealer contacts stay viewable with no signal.
  • Customizable dashboard, Dark/Light themes, and adjustable text size.

Your data, your control

  • Everything lives in your own Google Sheet and Drive — you own it outright, no lock-in, nothing held hostage on someone else's server.
  • Automatic backups with one-click restore, plus full CSV export anytime.
Put it on your phone

Installs in 10 seconds — no app store needed

On Android

  • 1. Open therailroster.com in Chrome and tap Open the App (sign in with Google).
  • 2. Tap Chrome's ⋮ menu in the top-right corner.
  • 3. Tap "Add to Home screen" (or "Install app") and confirm. Done — the conductor is on your home screen.

On iPhone or iPad

  • 1. Open therailroster.com in Safari and tap Open the App (sign in with Google).
  • 2. Tap the Share button (the square with the arrow, bottom of the screen).
  • 3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen," then Add. Done.

No lock-in. Ever.

Your roster is a Google Sheet in your own account. Your photos are in your own Drive. Cancel any time and everything stays yours — readable, exportable, and printable without us.
