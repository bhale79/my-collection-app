Questions

Good questions

Which trains are in the catalog?
Lionel prewar, postwar and modern (including HO and parts), American Flyer, MTH, Atlas, K-Line, Williams, Weaver, RMT, Marx, Menards, Bachmann, Kato, Märklin, LGB and more — over 160,000+ items, and it keeps growing. Something not in it? Add it by hand in a minute.
Do I need a Google account?
Yes — you sign in with Google, and that is where your list and photos are kept, in your own account. No Gmail address? Get one free from Google in about five minutes.
Phone or computer?
Both. It runs in your web browser on an iPhone, Android phone, tablet or computer, and puts an icon on your home screen like any other app — no app store needed. Sign in on each device and it’s the same collection everywhere.
I don’t know what I have. Can it help?
That’s what Photo ID is for: take a picture of the piece or its box and it suggests the item number for you to confirm. Printed labels can be read too, and box barcodes where the box has one on file.
I already keep my list in Excel.
Bring it with you — import your spreadsheet in minutes instead of typing it all again.
How does the free trial work?
Sign in with Google, then start your 3-week free trial. A card is needed to start it, but nothing is charged until the trial ends — cancel any time before then and you pay nothing. After that it’s , renewing yearly, with an email before each renewal.
What if I cancel?
You keep full use until the end of the year you paid for. There are no partial-year refunds. Your list and photos never leave your Google account, so they are always yours to open, print and export.
Is my collection private?
Your list and photos live in your own Google account, not on our servers. Nobody sees them unless you share them. Details in our Privacy Policy.
Who’s behind it?
A collector who wanted a better way to keep track of his own trains. Questions or ideas: support@therailroster.com — a real person answers.
